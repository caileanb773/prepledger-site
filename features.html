WHAT IT DOES

Everything you need to organize your recipes.

PrepLedger gives chefs and home cooks a simple way to create, organize, and manage their recipes from one place.

◆

Recipe Management

Keep your recipes organized and easy to work with.

  • Create and edit recipes
  • Manage ingredients, amounts, units, and notes
  • Add directions and tags
  • Add recipe images
  • Switch between grid and list views
  • Search and filter your recipes
↔

Import & Export

Keep your recipes portable and easy to back up.

  • Import recipes from JSON
  • Export recipes to JSON
  • Export individual recipes or your entire collection
  • Move recipes between PrepLedger installations
⚙

Desktop Experience

A focused desktop application built for everyday use.

  • Native Windows application
  • Light and dark appearance
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Local recipe storage
